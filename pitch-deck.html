THE MISSING METRIC

Recovery Debt

Recovery Debt = OCH Score - Biological Score

Rootly workload minus sleep + HRV recovery, modeled as a time-series signal

STABLE < 15 WARNING 15-30 CRITICAL > 30

This metric doesn't exist anywhere else.

~40s. "So we created Recovery Debt. It's simple: take Rootly's OCH score, which measures how heavy your workload is, and subtract a biological score we compute from sleep quality and heart rate variability. If the gap is small, you're stable. If it's growing, you're in warning. Over 30, you're in biological debt - your body can't repay what your schedule is taking. We track this over time, not just a snapshot. This metric doesn't exist anywhere else."

Thu 3AM Auth 5xx (sev1) Sleep: 54% UNRECOVERED
"INC-1838 (Sev0, 3AM) cost 64 hours of recovery. You've had 56 available. You're in deficit."

After-hours incidents cost 1.5x more recovery.

~45s. THIS IS YOUR STRONGEST SLIDE. Slow down here. "This is where it gets real. Every incident has a biological cost. Monday 2AM, a sev1 DB outage - that person's sleep efficiency dropped to 62%, and they needed 48 hours to recover. Wednesday daytime, a sev2 - sleep was fine, recovered in 4 hours. But Thursday 3AM, another sev1 - sleep efficiency 54%, and they never recovered. That's the key insight: after-hours incidents cost 1.5x more recovery. INC-1838, a sev0 at 3AM, cost 64 hours of biological recovery. The person only had 56 available. They're in deficit."
CLOSING THE LOOP

The Circuit Breaker

Assisted relief via Rootly API. Human stays in control.

Generate Smart Handoff

Rootly MCP: get_oncall_handoff_summary

AI drafts Slack-ready handoff with context

Request Shift Override

POST /v1/override_shifts

Finds backup, creates override shift

Silence Non-Critical

Rootly MCP: update alerts

Mutes sev3/sev4 for recovery period

Escalate to Backup

POST /v1/escalation_policies

Pages backup, moves you to secondary

Makes it socially safe to step away. Backed by data, not "I feel tired."

~40s. "But we don't just show data. We close the loop. The Circuit Breaker gives you four actions, all through Rootly's API. Generate a smart handoff - AI writes a Slack-ready summary of your open incidents so someone can pick up where you left off. Request a shift override. Silence non-critical alerts. Or escalate to backup. The human stays in control - we generate recommendations, you approve. The point is: the handoff button makes it socially safe to step away. It's backed by objective biological data, not just 'I feel tired'."
PRIVACY & SAFETY

Built to protect engineers, not evaluate them.

On-Device Only
Heart rate, sleep stages, HRV, fragmentation - never leaves the phone
Shared (Opt-in)
Readiness score, recovery delta, availability status - user controls
  • Cannot be used for performance reviews
  • Signal is advisory, not authoritative - human approval for all actions
  • DREAMT used as proxy dataset, calibrated per user in production
~30s. "Quick note on privacy. All biological data - heart rate, sleep stages, HRV - stays on the phone. Never leaves the device. What gets shared, only if you opt in, is an aggregate readiness score. That's it. This system is designed to protect people, not evaluate their performance. It can't be used for reviews. All actions are advisory - you always approve."
